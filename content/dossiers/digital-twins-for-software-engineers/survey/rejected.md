# Rejected candidates

<!-- Retrieved, read, and turned down. Recording these is what stops the
     next revision re-searching and re-judging the same papers -- it is
     the single most expensive thing a fresh session repeats. -->

Every entry below is a real citekey in this ledger. A rejection is a
statement about *this draft's* scope and this reader, never about the
paper's quality -- several of these are cited by the thesis chapter and
the deep-research briefing on the same topic.

| citekey | query that surfaced it | why rejected |
|---|---|---|
| `grieves_digital_2017` | digital twin definition | Origin/PLM framing. §1 is organised around the coupling criterion, not the concept's history; a survey that opens on provenance buries the distinction this reader needs |
| `tao_five-dimension_2019` | digital twin definition; digital twin architecture | Extends the origin framing to five dimensions. Same reason as above, and the added dimensions are modelling concerns the scope excludes |
| `pfeiffer_towards_2025` | digital twin definition variance | Third source for "definitions vary", after `vanderhorn_digital_2021` and `semeraro_digital_2021` already carried it. Kept out to avoid a three-citation pile on one uncontested claim |
| `liu_review_2021` | digital twin vs simulation | Review-of-reviews altitude. §1 needed a criterion to apply to the pot, not another survey layer |
| `shao_analysis_2023` | digital twin standards; reference architecture | ISO 23247 / standardisation scope. Real and relevant to the field, but standards conformance is not a question this reader is being asked |
| `heaton_platform_nodate` | digital twin platform architecture | Layered-stack description that §2's kept sources already establish, and `nodate` means no year to place it against the rest |
| `almeida_middleware_2023` | digital twin middleware | Middleware survey. `bellavista_entanglement-aware_2024` was kept for the same territory because it states the data-vs-process split §2 is built on |
| `tao_maketwin_2024` | digital twin platform; twin construction cost | Platform paper with low-code configuration as a headline. §3's platform slot went to the DTaaS line, which the reader can compose against |
| `michael_model-driven_2025` | model-driven engineering digital twin | MDE as the response to twin construction. `lehner_pattern_2023` already carries MDE into §2 as instantiable patterns; a second MDE argument drifts the survey toward advocacy |
| `combemale_model-based_2023` | DevOps digital twin; model-based operations | Model-based DevOps generally. §5 needed the twin-specific instance, which is `hugues_twinops_2022` |
| `combemale_challenges_2025` | digital twin systems of systems | Systems-of-systems integration framing. Legitimate, but multi-twin integration is a scale beyond one pot and one pump |
| `michael_integration_2022` | digital twin integration | Same territory as above, same reason |
| `frasheri_advanced_2024` | digital twin services catalogue | Service-catalogue framing (monitoring, prediction, decision support). Useful for a thesis narrative; §3 is about how you obtain a twin, not what it emits |
| `alskaif_evolution_2025` | digital twin evolution | Evolution as central to twin engineering. §5's evolution slot went to `mertens_continuous_2024` and `beaumont_towards_2025`, which are the notational and lifecycle-automation ends of it |
| `ali_modeling_2024` | digital twin novelty; modelling and simulation | Asks whether twins are evolution or revolution. That is a positional question, and taking a side on it is the thesis chapter's job, not a survey's |

| `thelen_comprehensive_2022` | digital twin model updating | Online model updating -- refitting a model from data collected while the system runs. Squarely inside "the physics inside the models", which the scope excludes |
| `zampetti_continuous_2023` | continuous integration cyber-physical | Genuinely close to §5 and the hardest rejection here: CI practice for cyber-physical teams, hardware-in-the-loop included. Turned down only because `barbie_toward_2024` covers twin CI specifically and ships replication artefacts. **Reconsider this one first if §5 is ever expanded** |
| `feng_model-based_2023` | sensor filtering digital twin | Kalman filtering for state estimation. Model internals; excluded by scope, and it belongs to the tutorial draft's "where to go next" rather than here |
